About hospital leadership

Hospital leadership brings together clinical expertise, operational coordination and responsible stewardship of public resources. At a major referral hospital, these responsibilities support patient care, staff coordination, service continuity and collaboration with the wider health system.

!

Official information notice: This page describes leadership functions in general terms. It does not identify current office-holders or confirm ANGAU’s approved leadership hierarchy. Current names, titles, appointment dates and reporting lines should be verified with the hospital administration or the responsible Papua New Guinea health authority before publication.

Key leadership responsibilities

Responsibilities may be distributed across executive, clinical, nursing, administrative and departmental leaders according to the hospital’s approved structure and applicable government arrangements.

✚

Clinical leadership

Support clinical standards, coordination between specialties, evidence-informed practice and continuous improvement in the quality and safety of patient care.

♡

Nursing leadership

Coordinate nursing practice, workforce support, professional standards and safe delivery of nursing care across relevant services.

▦

Operations and administration

Coordinate facilities, logistics, administrative processes, service planning and the operational support needed for hospital activities.

♙

People and workforce

Support staff development, appropriate supervision, workplace communication, recruitment processes and a respectful professional environment.

▤

Finance and resources

Promote accountable use of public funds, assets and supplies in line with approved budgets, procurement rules and delegated responsibilities.

◎

Partnerships and public service

Maintain appropriate working relationships with health authorities, referral providers, training institutions, communities and development partners.

Leadership principles

Effective public hospital leadership is supported by clear responsibilities, timely communication, respect for patients and staff, evidence-informed decisions and transparent accountability.

●
Patient-centred decisionsConsider patient dignity, safety, access to care and continuity of services.
●
Team coordinationEncourage collaboration across clinical, nursing, technical and administrative teams.
●
AccountabilityEnsure responsibilities are clear and decisions follow applicable policies and delegated authority.
●
Continuous improvementUse appropriate feedback, service information and review processes to identify opportunities to improve care.

Leadership directory

A public directory should only be populated with confirmed, approved information. When validated details are available, it may include an office or role title, official responsibilities, public-facing contact channel and the date the information was last checked.

Do not publish unverified names, personal phone numbers, private email addresses, staff rosters or internal reporting information. Official contact routes should be used for enquiries and requests to reach the relevant office.
