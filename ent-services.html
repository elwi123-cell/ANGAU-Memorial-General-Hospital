Clinical Service

Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT)

Assessment, diagnosis, treatment and referral pathways for conditions affecting the ear, nose, throat and related head and neck structures.

Patient Information Appointments & Referrals
Before you attend

Confirm current clinic arrangements, referral requirements, operating times, fees and any preparation instructions before travelling to the hospital.

Important: Serious breathing difficulties, significant bleeding, major facial or airway injuries, or other urgent symptoms may require immediate emergency assessment. Use verified hospital emergency information.
What the service covers

ENT and related care

ENT care may involve assessment of ear, nose and throat symptoms, diagnosis, treatment planning, monitoring and referral according to individual clinical needs.

Ear conditions

Assessment and management of ear-related symptoms and conditions, including hearing or balance concerns where clinically appropriate.

Nose and sinus care

Evaluation and treatment planning for nasal, sinus and related upper-airway conditions according to clinical assessment.

Throat and head & neck

Assessment of throat and related head and neck concerns, with appropriate investigation, treatment or referral.

Care pathway

From assessment to follow-up

01

Clinical assessment

Symptoms, history and relevant clinical findings are assessed to determine the appropriate next step in care.

02

Investigation & treatment

Diagnostic support and treatment are coordinated where required and according to available clinical services.

03

Review & referral

Patients may receive follow-up, monitoring or referral for additional specialist care when clinically indicated.

Related clinical support

Coordinated hospital care

ENT patients may require support from other hospital services for diagnosis, medicines, surgery, anaesthesia, emergency care or management of related medical conditions.

  • Medical assessment and diagnostic services
  • Medical imaging and pathology support where clinically indicated
  • Pharmacy and medication support
  • Surgical and anaesthetic services where required
  • Referral and rehabilitation pathways where appropriate
Patient preparation

Plan your visit

Before attending, confirm whether a referral or appointment is required and bring relevant clinical information, referral documents and medicines where appropriate.

  • Confirm clinic location and operating arrangements
  • Check referral or appointment requirements
  • Ask about fees, payments and eligibility arrangements
  • Bring previous results or relevant clinical records if available
  • Follow any preparation instructions provided by the clinical team
