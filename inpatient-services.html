Inpatient Services overview

Inpatient services support patients who require hospital-based treatment, observation, nursing care, procedures or recovery that cannot be provided solely through an outpatient visit, subject to clinical assessment and bed availability.

What inpatient care may involve

The exact care pathway depends on the patient's condition, clinical needs, treatment plan and the hospital's current arrangements.

●
Clinical assessmentHealthcare professionals assess the patient's condition and determine the appropriate level of care.
●
Nursing and monitoringWard teams provide ongoing nursing care and monitor patients according to their clinical needs.
●
Investigations and treatmentPatients may receive investigations, medicines, procedures or other treatment recommended by the clinical team.
●
Care coordinationDifferent clinical departments and support services may work together to coordinate a patient's care.
