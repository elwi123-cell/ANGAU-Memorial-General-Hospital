Hospital identity and regional role

Hospital Profile

ANGAU Memorial General Hospital is a major public referral hospital in Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Its role includes serving the local community, supporting provincial health services and receiving referrals from the wider Momase region and beyond.

Institutional background

The name ANGAU refers to the Australia New Guinea Administrative Unit, an organisation established during the Second World War. Publicly available hospital background material records that the facility was officially opened at its Lae site on 7 April 1964, following relocation from Malahang.

Over time, ANGAU developed into a major referral institution for Lae and Morobe, with patients also referred from other parts of Papua New Guinea. Its location and transport connections contribute to its regional significance.

The hospital has been administered within the provincial health system. Public audit reporting records that the Morobe Provincial Health Authority became fully functional with its Board on 20 June 2019 under the Provincial Health Authorities Act 2007. For current governance and leadership details, consult the Authority’s latest official communications.

Facilities documented in redevelopment sources

PNG Treasury, Australian Government and project-delivery sources report completion of major redevelopment components by June 2024. The list below reflects facilities described in those documents; it is not a real-time directory or confirmation that each unit is open under the same arrangements today.

  • Children’s outpatient and emergency services
  • Paediatric inpatient ward
  • Maternity and labour ward
  • Special care nursery
  • Intensive care ward
  • Operating theatre
  • Pathology services
  • X-ray and medical imaging
  • Tuberculosis ward and clinic
  • Infectious disease inpatient unit
  • Central sterilisation department
  • Family Support Centre
  • Kitchen, storage and laundry facilities
  • Waste management and energy infrastructure
  • Mortuary and transit ward

Redevelopment and ongoing development

The hospital redevelopment was delivered through cooperation between the Governments of Papua New Guinea and Australia. Public project records describe construction beginning in 2018 and major redevelopment works reaching completion in 2022, with project components subsequently reported as completed by June 2024. In June 2026, the Australian High Commission announced the start of construction of a new pharmacy intended to strengthen medicine management and dispensing for patients across Morobe and the Momase region; completion was expected in late 2026.

These are project milestones reported by public sources. For current project status, clinical operating arrangements, contact details and patient instructions, check the latest announcements from MPHA or the hospital.
