Surgical Services overview

Surgical services may include assessment, operative procedures and perioperative care for patients referred or admitted for surgical management, subject to clinical need, specialist availability and hospital capacity.

Typical surgical care pathway

The exact pathway varies according to the patient's condition, procedure and clinical circumstances.

●
Referral or presentationA patient may reach surgical services through referral, outpatient assessment, inpatient care or emergency presentation.
●
Clinical assessmentThe surgical team assesses the patient's condition and determines the appropriate management plan.
●
Preparation and consentWhere appropriate, patients receive procedure-specific preparation and information from authorised clinical staff.
●
Procedure and recoverySurgery and subsequent recovery are managed by the relevant clinical teams according to hospital protocols.

Related surgical support

Surgical patients may interact with several hospital services depending on their clinical needs.
