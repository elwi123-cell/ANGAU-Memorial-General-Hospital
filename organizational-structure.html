Organising hospital services

ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital provides healthcare services in Lae, Morobe Province. A hospital organisational structure typically sets out leadership responsibilities, clinical departments, nursing services, diagnostic and treatment functions, and the administrative and operational teams that support care delivery.

Why a clear structure matters

●
Clear responsibilityStaff and patients can better understand which office or service is responsible for an enquiry or operational decision.
●
Coordinated patient careDefined relationships between clinical teams and support services help maintain continuity and safe care.
●
Accountable decisionsDocumented reporting lines support appropriate supervision, escalation and review.
●
Service planningOrganisational information helps identify workforce, equipment, infrastructure and training needs.

Requesting an official organogram

For an authoritative structure chart, request the current approved organogram or organisational chart from the hospital administration or relevant health authority. Confirm the document date, approval status and whether it is suitable for public release.

Internal-only reporting lines, staff personal information and restricted operational details should not be published without appropriate authorisation.
