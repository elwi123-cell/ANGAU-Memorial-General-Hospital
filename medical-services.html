About hospital medical services

ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital is a public hospital in Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea, and an important healthcare facility for the region. Hospital services and referral arrangements may change as staffing, equipment, facilities and redevelopment activities evolve.

!

Please confirm availability: The service categories below are an informational overview, not a real-time service directory. They do not guarantee that a particular clinic, specialist, procedure or diagnostic service is currently operating. For current availability, referral requirements, clinic times and admission arrangements, contact the hospital or the relevant health service directly. In a life-threatening emergency, seek urgent local emergency assistance rather than waiting for a website response.

Before you attend

To help your visit proceed smoothly, contact the relevant clinic or hospital office to confirm current arrangements. Where applicable, bring:

●
Referral and appointment detailsBring your referral letter, appointment information or clinic instructions if you have them.
●
Relevant health informationTell the clinical team about current medicines, allergies and important medical history.
●
Identification and recordsBring identification and previous test results or health records if requested by the service.
●
Practical arrangementsConfirm fees, transport, visiting rules and whether a support person may accompany you.

Patient information and referrals

Patients may need to follow a referral pathway or complete registration before receiving planned services. Requirements can vary by clinic and clinical circumstances.

This website does not diagnose conditions, provide individual treatment instructions or replace advice from a qualified health professional. If symptoms are severe or rapidly worsening, seek urgent medical assessment.
