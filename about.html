About ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital

ANGAU is located in Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. Public project documentation describes it as a district hospital for Lae, a provincial hospital for Morobe, and a regional referral facility for the Momase region.

Location and service area

Purpose and regional importance

ANGAU Memorial Provincial Hospital plays an important role in the public health system of Morobe Province. Its location in Lae supports access to hospital-level care in a major population and transport centre.

The Papua New Guinea and Australian governments established a programme of cooperation to redevelop the hospital’s infrastructure. The programme was planned in stages so that existing healthcare services could continue while facilities were upgraded and new buildings were commissioned.

Redevelopment and facilities

PNG Treasury’s Public Investment Program records completed redevelopment components including the pathology facility, operating theatre, paediatric ward, special care nursery, maternity and labour ward, intensive care ward, TB facilities, imaging, central sterilisation, infectious disease inpatient unit and emergency/outpatient areas.

Published redevelopment information is evidence of planned or completed infrastructure, not a live service directory. Clinic hours, eligibility, referral rules, staffing and current service availability should be confirmed directly with the hospital or Morobe Provincial Health Authority before being published as current facts.

Development background

A bilateral Joint Understanding on cooperation in health, education and law and order was agreed by Papua New Guinea and Australia in July 2013. Planning for the ANGAU redevelopment followed, including a health services and facilities master plan completed in 2015.

Project information published by the Australian Government describes major redevelopment support during 2017–2024. In June 2026, the Australian High Commission reported the start of construction on a new pharmacy intended to improve medicine management and dispensing services. That announcement stated that completion was expected in late 2026; readers should consult current official updates for the latest status.

Our public-information approach

This website is designed to help patients, families, healthcare workers, researchers, suppliers and development partners find hospital information in one accessible place.

For patient safety and accuracy, information about clinical services, staff, fees, appointment procedures, visiting arrangements, emergency contacts and official notices must be verified with authorised hospital representatives before publication. This website is not an emergency dispatch service and should not be used to submit confidential medical information.
